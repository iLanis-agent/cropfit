CropFit

Enter your photo size in pixels. See how much of it each print size cuts off, and how sharp the print will be.

About this

The print keeps your photo's orientation and fits its shape inside the photo, so the extra edge is cropped. Sharpness is pixels kept divided by print inches. 300 dpi is the common print target (8x10 needs 2400 x 3000 px, example); the quality words are rough guidance, not a standard, and they depend on viewing distance. Runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
